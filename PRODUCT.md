# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output) deployed on Vercel from GitHub `gbrprill/gofit`. Chosen by the user on 2026-09-28 over Next.js. GSAP + ScrollTrigger for motion. Self-hosted fonts via Fontsource.

## Users

People in the Sudoeste do Paraná (Realeza, Francisco Beltrão, Pato Branco) deciding where to train, mostly arriving from Instagram on a phone. Three recurring profiles: people who want results with guidance, people who lift heavy and judge a gym by its equipment, and beginners who need to know what the first visit looks like. Personal trainers also choose where to bring clients.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for GOFIT Premium Gym, a three-unit training center chain. Success is a visitor picking a unit and opening a WhatsApp conversation with that unit.

## Positioning

A premium CT (centro de treinamento) in the Sudoeste do Paraná: named equipment (Panatta from Italy and Real Leader USA in Pato Branco), serious training culture, three units under one standard. Not a wellness spa, not a low-cost chain.

## Operating Context

Conversion happens in WhatsApp, one conversation per unit. Instagram profiles: @gofit_realeza, @gofitfb, @gofit_patobranco. Founder Marcos Vinicius Ciserza. Units: Realeza (since 2016, Rua Belém 2454), Francisco Beltrão (since 2023, Av. Luiz Antonio Faedo 1922), Pato Branco (since Nov 2025, Av. Tupi 1644).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Verified: modalities per unit as seen on Instagram; ~60 parking spots, locker rooms with showers and physical assessment in Francisco Beltrão; Panatta and Real Leader USA in Pato Branco; mascot Sheriff in Realeza. Realeza also has scent marketing, but the user asked (2026-09-29) never to mention it on the site.
- Undecided (must not appear as fact until the client confirms): WhatsApp numbers, opening hours, whether a free trial class exists, prices and plans, staff names and CREF, the 4-step method, equipment brands in Realeza and Francisco Beltrão, whether the student plan is still active.
- Primary action wording is neutral ("Agendar visita") until the trial class is confirmed.

## Brand Commitments

- Logo: "GO" white + "FIT" yellow, yellow bar with 5 black stars, "PREMIUM GYM" with a three-bar E. Colors #000, #FFF, #FFCC2A. The user pinned these colors and fonts similar to the logo's geometric heavy sans.
- Spirit: CT de treinamento premium. The user said "não fuja dessa linha".
- Voice: direct, short sentences, a demanding but respectful coach.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: logo PNG, Instagram posts and follower counts (Sep 2026), CNPJ records, public comments ("Estrutura padrão ouro", "Um sonho de academia", "Impecável").
- Not on hand: real photos or video in high resolution, testimonials with consent, staff photos. Current imagery is 3D renders made for the brand; never present them as the real units.

## Product Principles

1. Every button ends in the right unit's WhatsApp.
2. Prove with named equipment and real facts; never invent numbers, people or offers.
3. Mobile first: the Instagram in-app browser on 4G is the main context.
4. Premium means restraint: black ground, yellow as a signal, nothing that looks like a promotion flyer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast (yellow never on white), full keyboard support, prefers-reduced-motion path for every animation.
